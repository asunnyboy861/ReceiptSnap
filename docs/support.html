❓ Frequently Asked Questions

  • How many free scans do I get? — You get 10 free scans per month. Upgrade to Premium for unlimited scans.
  • Does the app work offline? — Yes! OCR processing happens entirely on your device. No internet required.
  • How do I export my receipts? — Go to the Receipts tab, tap the export button, and choose CSV format.
  • Does it sync across devices? — Premium users get iCloud sync across all their Apple devices.
  • How do I cancel my subscription? — Go to Settings → Your Name → Subscriptions on your iPhone, or use the App Store.

🔄 Subscription Management

To manage or cancel your subscription:

  • Open the Settings app on your iPhone
  • Tap your name at the top
  • Tap Subscriptions
  • Select ReceiptSnap
  • Choose to cancel or change your plan

📱 System Requirements

  • iPhone or iPad running iOS 17.0 or later
  • Camera access for scanning receipts
  • iCloud account for sync (Premium)
