Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 7, 2026

1. Overview

ZZoutuo ("we," "our," or "us") built the ReceiptSnap app as a commercial service. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our app.

2. Information We Collect

Information You Provide:

  • Receipt images you scan using the camera
  • OCR-extracted data (merchant name, total, tax, date)
  • Notes you add to receipts
  • Contact information when you submit support requests

Information Collected Automatically:

  • Device model and iOS version (for crash reporting)
  • App usage analytics (anonymized, aggregated only)

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and improve the receipt scanning service
  • To process in-app purchases and manage subscriptions
  • To send smart reminders you've opted into
  • To respond to support requests
  • To improve app performance and user experience

4. On-Device Processing

All OCR text recognition is performed entirely on your device. Receipt images are processed locally using Apple's Vision framework. Your receipt images and extracted text are never uploaded to our servers for OCR processing.

5. Data Storage & iCloud Sync

Receipt data is stored locally on your device using SwiftData. If you enable iCloud Sync (Premium feature), your data is encrypted and stored in your personal iCloud account via CloudKit. We do not have access to your iCloud-synced data.

6. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. We only share data in the following circumstances:

  • With Apple for App Store purchase processing
  • When required by law or legal process
  • To protect our rights or prevent fraud

7. Location Data

If you enable Smart Reminders, we use your device's location to provide store-proximity reminders. Location data is processed on-device only and is never stored on our servers. You can disable location access at any time in your device Settings.

8. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your personal data stored in the app
  • Delete your data by deleting receipts within the app
  • Export your data using the CSV export feature
  • Revoke location and camera permissions via device Settings
  • Cancel your subscription at any time

9. Children's Privacy

ReceiptSnap is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

10. Data Retention

Your receipt data remains on your device until you delete it. If you delete the app, all locally stored data is permanently removed. iCloud-synced data can be managed through your iCloud settings.

11. Security

We implement industry-standard security measures. On-device processing, iCloud encryption, and secure API communications protect your financial data.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
